How to play

Drag a shape onto the board, or tap a shape and then tap where its top-left corner should go.

Fill a complete row or column to clear it. Use all three shapes to get a fresh set. The game ends when none of your pieces fit.

On iPhone, choose Share → Add to Home Screen to play like an app.
