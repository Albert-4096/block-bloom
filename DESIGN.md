# Block Bloom design direction

Mode: Operate. The player should understand the board, the three available pieces, and the next action at a glance.

The game feels like a bright tabletop object: cream paper, soft ink, a midnight-blue inset board, and jewel-colored tiles with an enamel highlight. Expressive Bricolage Grotesque headings pair with plain DM Sans controls. Rounded geometry and directional shadows make blocks feel touchable without visual noise. The first screen is almost entirely playable game; instructions stay secondary. On phones the board occupies the available width and the tray sits in the thumb zone. On wider screens the game sits beside a quiet rules and status rail.

Colors: paper `#f8f4e9`, ink `#26344b`, board `#203657`, teal `#4bbfbd`, coral `#f27d72`, gold `#efb94c`, violet `#a78bd8`, mint `#89c994`.

Motion: a short placement press and line-clear glow; honor reduced-motion. Use visible focus and large hit targets. Text and controls retain strong contrast over the warm background.
